# Dealer interview guide (car-rental offices)

**Goal:** confirm that dealers will keep availability fresh, find out how they take bookings today, and
learn what they would pay for. 30–40 minutes, in Egyptian Arabic, at the office where possible
(watch how the phone and WhatsApp are used). Target: 12–15 offices across Cairo, Giza and Alexandria,
mixed by fleet size (1–5, 6–20, 20+ cars).

Consent: explain the purpose, ask to record audio, and say that nothing is published with the office's
name. Offer a small thank-you (e.g. three months of Pro free at launch).

## Warm-up (5 min)

- D1. How long has the office been open? How many cars? Owned, or managed for owners?
- D2. Who answers the phone and WhatsApp? Opening hours in practice?

## How customers find you today (10 min)

- D3. Where do new customers come from? (Facebook groups/pages, Instagram, OLX/Dubizzle, Google Maps,
  repeat customers, hotels/drivers.) Rough share of each?
- D4. Walk me through the last booking from the first message to the handover.
- D5. How many enquiries a day, and how many turn into a rental? What happens to the rest?
- D6. How much time goes on "is it available?" messages about cars that are already out?

## Availability and pricing (10 min) — the core risk

- D7. How do you know which car is free tomorrow? (Notebook, Excel, memory, an app.) Show me.
- D8. If an app asked you to tap "still available" every 1–2 days, would you? What would stop you?
  What reminder would work best: WhatsApp, a push notification, a call?
- D9. How do you price: per day, week, month? Do you change prices by season or for regulars?
- D10. Deposit, licence, ID, age and insurance requirements? Which documents do you keep?

## Trust and verification (5 min)

- D11. Would you share your commercial registration and tax card to get a "verified" badge?
  Any concern about uploading them?
- D12. Bad experiences with customers (damage, no-shows, fraud)? What protects you today?
- D13. How do you feel about public reviews? What would make them fair?

## Willingness to pay (5 min)

- D14. What do you spend today on getting customers (ads, commissions, boosts)?
- D15. If it brought you N extra confirmed rentals a month, what monthly fee would feel fair?
  React to: free / 1,499 EGP Pro / 3,999 EGP Fleet; 50 EGP/day featured listing.
- D16. Would you prefer a monthly fee or a commission per rental? Why?

## Close

- D17. Anything we should have asked? May we come back with a prototype?

## Notes template

Office · Area · Fleet size · Channels · Availability method · Would reconfirm (Y/N/why) ·
Price point reaction · Quotes worth keeping · Follow-up.
