# Customer interview guide (people renting a car)

**Goal:** understand how people search for a rental today, what makes them trust an office, and which
details they need before calling. 25–30 minutes, Arabic or English. Target: 12–15 people who rented
a car in the last 12 months: residents, Egyptians abroad visiting home, and foreign residents/tourists.

Consent as in the dealer guide. Incentive: a 200 EGP voucher.

## Context (5 min)

- C1. When did you last rent a car? Where, for how long, for what?
- C2. How did you find the office? What did you try first?

## Search and comparison (10 min)

- C3. Walk me through how you compared options. What did you write down or screenshot?
- C4. What information did you need before contacting anyone? (Price, deposit, insurance, licence
  rules, age, delivery, mileage limits, photos of the actual car.)
- C5. Did you ever call about a car that turned out to be unavailable? How did it feel?
- C6. Phone call, WhatsApp, or a form? Why?
- C7. Prices and phone numbers: do you prefer to read digits as 123 or ١٢٣? (Informs ADR-0009.)

## Trust (7 min)

- C8. What made you trust or distrust an office? Would a "verified business" badge change that?
- C9. Do you read reviews for rentals? Where? What makes a review believable?
- C10. Have you had a bad experience (hidden fees, deposit not returned, a different car)?

## Product reactions (5 min) — show the prototype

- C11. Look at this listing page. What is missing? What would you tap first?
- C12. "Availability confirmed 3 hours ago": does this help? Would "7 days ago" put you off?
- C13. Would you sign in with your phone number to contact an office? Google or Apple instead?

## Close

- C14. Anything else? May we contact you for a usability test?
