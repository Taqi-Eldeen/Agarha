# Personal data map

Every table or store that holds personal data, why, and for how long. Lawful bases under PDPL
(Law 151/2020): **C** contract / service delivery, **LI** legitimate interest, **Co** consent,
**L** legal obligation. Export and erase are implemented per module through `PrivacyContributor`
(`apps/api/src/infra/privacy.ts`); identity orchestrates `GET /v1/me/export` and `DELETE /v1/me`.

| Store / table                                 | Personal data                                                  | Purpose                                                | Basis                                 | Retention                                             | On account deletion                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `users`                                       | phone (E.164), display name, locale                            | Sign-in, contact with dealers                          | C                                     | Life of account                                       | Anonymised immediately (phone and name nulled)          |
| `user_identities`                             | Google/Apple subject, email                                    | Social sign-in                                         | C                                     | Life of account                                       | Deleted                                                 |
| `user_credentials`                            | password hash (argon2id), TOTP secret (AES-GCM)                | Dealer/admin sign-in                                   | C                                     | Life of account                                       | Deleted                                                 |
| `otp_challenges`                              | phone, HMAC of code, HMAC of IP                                | OTP verification, abuse limits                         | C, LI                                 | 24 h (purged by the daily retention job)              | Deleted                                                 |
| `refresh_tokens`                              | token hash, user agent, HMAC of IP                             | Sessions, "your devices"                               | C, LI                                 | Until 7 days after expiry (≤ 180-day family cap)      | Revoked and deleted                                     |
| `dealers`, `branches`, `dealer_members`       | business contacts, branch addresses/coordinates, staff phones  | Public dealer profile                                  | C                                     | Life of dealer account                                | Staff membership removed; business record kept (B2B)    |
| `verification_docs` + R2 `private-docs`       | CR, tax card, owner national ID images                         | Dealer verification                                    | L, LI                                 | Rejected: 90 days; approved: while active + 24 months | Purged per schedule                                     |
| `leads`, `availability_requests`              | user id, customer phone, note, HMAC of IP                      | Connect customer and dealer, response rate             | C                                     | 24 months                                             | User unlinked; lead kept for the dealer until retention |
| `reviews`                                     | rating, text, author name                                      | Trust                                                  | C, LI                                 | Life of review                                        | Kept, author shown as "former user"                     |
| `reports`                                     | reporter id, free text                                         | Moderation                                             | LI                                    | 24 months                                             | Reporter unlinked                                       |
| `favorites`, `saved_searches`                 | user id, search criteria                                       | Customer features                                      | C                                     | Life of account                                       | Deleted                                                 |
| `push_tokens`                                 | Expo push token, platform                                      | Push notifications                                     | C                                     | Until invalid/uninstall                               | Deleted                                                 |
| `notification_preferences`                    | per-topic channel toggles (marketing is opt-in)                | Consent management                                     | Co                                    | Life of account                                       | Deleted                                                 |
| `notification_deliveries`                     | recipient (phone/email/token), template, status                | Delivery, failover, OTP metrics                        | C, LI                                 | 90 days                                               | Recipient purged with schedule                          |
| `audit_log`                                   | actor id, action, HMAC of IP                                   | Security, accountability for admin access to documents | L, LI                                 | 24 months                                             | Actor id kept (admins only)                             |
| `subscriptions`, `invoices`, `payment_events` | dealer billing contact, amounts; no card data                  | Billing, tax                                           | C, L                                  | 5 years (tax law)                                     | Kept (legal obligation)                                 |
| `listing_daily_stats`                         | none (aggregates; visitor keys are daily HMACs in Redis)       | Dealer analytics                                       | LI                                    | Life of listing                                       | n/a                                                     |
| Redis                                         | rate-limit counters, OTP resend timers, daily view de-dup keys | Abuse protection, analytics                            | LI                                    | Minutes to 24 h (TTL)                                 | Expire                                                  |
| CloudWatch logs                               | request logs with hashed user id, no bodies, no phone numbers  | Operations, security                                   | LI                                    | Production 90 days, staging 14 days                   | Expire                                                  |
| Sentry                                        | errors with hashed user id; PII scrubbing on                   | Debugging                                              | LI                                    | 30 days (Sentry plan)                                 | Expire                                                  |
| PostHog                                       | pseudonymous events, no phone or name                          | Product analytics                                      | LI (Co for mobile ATT where required) | 12 months                                             | Person deleted via API                                  |
| Twilio / Vonage / Meta                        | phone, message text                                            | SMS/WhatsApp delivery                                  | C                                     | Provider policy (DPA required)                        | n/a                                                     |

## Not collected

Customer national IDs or licences, card numbers (Paymob hosted checkout), precise location history
(only the current map viewport or "near me" coordinates for a single search request, not stored),
contacts, photo EXIF/GPS (stripped).
